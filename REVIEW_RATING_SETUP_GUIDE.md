# ⭐️ Simple Guide: Approve & Add Reviews in Shopify Admin Dashboard

This guide explains how customer reviews work on your store (**Shopifycosmatics**) and how you add or approve reviews **exclusively from your official Shopify Admin Dashboard** (`admin.shopify.com`).

---

## 🎯 How Review Approval Works (Example: Face Wash)

### 1️⃣ Customer Submits a Review
- A customer opens a product page (e.g. **Face Wash**).
- They click **Write a review**, select **5 Stars**, write *"Good product"*, and click **Submit**.
- 🔒 **Important**: The review does **NOT** show on your website automatically. It is sent as a submission to your store admin.

---

### 2️⃣ You Approve & Add the Review in Shopify Admin Dashboard

1. Log into your [Shopify Admin Panel](https://admin.shopify.com).
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Products**.
3. Select the product (e.g. **Face Wash** or **Shaving Cream**).
4. Scroll all the way down to the bottom of the page to **Metafields**.
5. Click on **Reviews List** (`custom.reviews_list`).
6. Click **`➕ Add new entry`**.
7. Enter the review details:
   - **Reviewer Name**: Customer name (e.g., `Prafful Sharma`)
   - **Rating**: `5`
   - **Title**: `Great face wash!`
   - **Body**: `Best face wash product ever used.`
8. Click **Save** on the entry form.
9. Click the black **Save** button in the top right corner of the Product page.

---

### 🌟 3️⃣ Review Displays Live on Your Website!

- Now open the product page on your store website (`/products/face-wash`).
- **Result**: The 5-star rating and approved customer review immediately show live on the product page for all customers!
